THE COSY GIRL EDIT

Three ways to serve a look from home

  1. Start with a soft base. Try an oversized tee, ribbed tank or comfy matching set.
  2. Pick one pop. Add a colourful hair clip, fun socks or a favourite necklace.
  3. Make it yours. Roll your sleeves, layer a cardigan or tie a ribbon in your hair.

The secret accessory is confidence. The second secret accessory is a snack.
