A LITTLE NOTE FROM THE EDITORS

ABOUT US!

A tiny corner of the internet with a big personality.

Welcome to Pepper Pop

Pepper Pop is a fictional early-2000s-inspired gossip and lifestyle magazine made for fun. Think glossy magazine covers, playful headlines, bubblegum colours, bedroom fashion experiments and quizzes to share with your besties.

Our imaginary newsroom publishes made-up celebrity stories only. Any names, quotes, events or rumours in the Hot Goss section are fictional and are not intended to describe real people.

Our look & feel

The design takes inspiration from the bright, layered energy of Y2K pop culture: sticker-like labels, bold display lettering, scrapbook-style panels and a pink, lilac and aqua palette. The layout is original and was written from scratch in HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
